This illustrative project demonstrates a portfolio page for a recording session. It is sample content that can be replaced with an actual session and its recordings.

## The creative direction

Keep the performance close and natural, preserving the interplay between voice and guitar rather than building a dense arrangement around it.

## The recording and mixing approach

- Plan a recording setup around the performer and the room.
- Select and edit takes while preserving the feel of the performance.
- Balance the vocal and guitar, using subtle processing to support clarity.

## What a finished story could include

Share session photos, notes on microphone choices, and an approved listening sample. Additional pages could cover the recording setup or compare stages of the mix.
