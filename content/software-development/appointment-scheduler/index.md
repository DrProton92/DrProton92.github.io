This illustrative project outlines a booking application for a small service business. It is an example of the kind of project story that can be published here, rather than a claim about a completed client engagement.

## The idea

Make it easier for customers to request a time while giving the business a clear view of its schedule.

## The approach

- A weekly calendar with available times and booked appointments.
- A simple booking flow that collects the information needed for the visit.
- Tools for adjusting availability and handling cancellations.
