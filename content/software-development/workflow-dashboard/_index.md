This illustrative project shows how a custom application could help a small team keep track of its work. It is sample portfolio content, ready to be replaced with a real project story.

## The idea

Tasks and deadlines often live in several different places. A shared dashboard could make the next steps visible without asking team members to assemble their own status reports.

## The approach

- A project overview with upcoming milestones and progress at a glance.
- Task lists organized by status, with owners and due dates.
- A layout that adapts from a desktop workspace to a phone screen.
